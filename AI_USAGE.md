# AI usage

No external LLM is enabled. Business question answering retrieves records from SQLite and quotes short evidence previews with source identifiers. Exact source counts use SQL. Document categories, email classes, and attachment flags use deterministic keyword rules that can be reviewed and replaced.

This is intentional: organizer permission for sending sandbox content to an external model is not yet known. If approval is later granted, a provider should be added behind a replaceable interface, limited to retrieved evidence, with no secrets or unrelated records in the prompt. WhatsApp summaries, request/commitment phrases, decisions, and action items are produced by local keyword and sentence heuristics, not an external model. Query results label them as candidates requiring human review; they do not establish that a decision or commitment is true, identify an owner or deadline, or validate the organizer's schema. The group-ID mapping and authorization list remain subject to organizer confirmation.

Cross-source search is deterministic. One complete double-quoted literal is matched as a case-sensitive, punctuation-sensitive substring (minimum three characters) in a stored title or content field; no entity validity or cross-source identity is inferred. When a record qualifies for both exact and topic matching, it is counted once as an exact literal match. Topic matches use the SQLite FTS5 index over titles and content and may reflect keyword overlap only. Per-source matching counts count stored records before display limits, not customers or transactions. WhatsApp results in these paths are restricted to the current selected-group allowlist, and are omitted when no groups are selected. Shared text across sources does not prove that records concern the same customer, order, or transaction.
